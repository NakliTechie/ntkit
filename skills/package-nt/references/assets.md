## Phase 2 — Marketing assets → `marketing/`

Write into `marketing/` and list what landed:
- **Screens.** Reuse the `/guide-nt` generator's captures if the repo has one; otherwise capture with Playwright. Headless has no WebGPU: capture WebGPU flows through Chrome.
- **Heroes.** The money screen, padded and sized per channel: `marketing/hero-x.png` (~1600×900), `marketing/hero-linkedin.png` (1200×627), `marketing/hero-square.png` (1080×1080), plus an optional montage or GIF.
- **Social cards.** Two 1280×640 cards from one template, per [`social-card-build.md`](social-card-build.md): the repo's (`marketing/social.png`, or the repo's existing card path) and, for a project with a deployed URL, the app's (`marketing/social-app.png`). Upload the new repo card per [`social-preview.md`](social-preview.md): `true` from the GraphQL check does not prove the current image, so re-upload after any re-render. The app card is the user's to ship.
- **Launch video.** `demo` or `promo`, made by the vendored brag, lands as `marketing/launch.mp4` + `marketing/launch.jpg`; procedure in [`launch-video.md`](launch-video.md). A failed precondition skips the video, listed under nice-to-haves with the reason; the video never blocks the run.
- **Frame 0 is the thumbnail.** X, Slack and Discord use frame 0 as a video's thumbnail. Bake the strongest *settled* frame (every line fully in, nothing mid-transition) into frame 0 of every video with `"$SKILL/references/bake-poster.sh" <video.mp4> <seconds>`, and build any GIF from the baked MP4.
- **Stranger test.** Once the hero, the cards and the video exist, brief a fresh-context subagent (Agent) with images only: `marketing/hero-x.png`, the repo card, and one still per video scene (`ffmpeg -ss <t> -i marketing/launch.mp4 -frames:v 1 <scene>.png`). No README, no repo, no pitch. It answers three lines: what this does · who it is for · how to get it. Compare the answers with the README's header sentence and install line. A wrong or "can't tell" on *what* or *who* → fix the tagline, the facts line, the hero framing or the video direction, then test again. Grade *how* on a `demo` video only: a card travels with its link, and a `promo` ends on the name by design.
- **Local, not committed.** Add `/marketing` and `/brag-output*` to `.gitignore` (no trailing slash; `git check-ignore -q marketing`). Promo output is regenerable; `/windup-nt` deletes it when no open item names it. A repo's existing card path stays committed. Social cards upload to GitHub; the app card is the user's to ship from `marketing/social-app.png` before windup sweeps it.
